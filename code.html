About the Hackathon

developer_board

Interdisciplinary Innovation

Civithon is the premier interdisciplinary hackathon of 2026, held as part of the Civitronic-Z club inauguration. Our aim is to bridge the gap between hardware, software, and structural engineering, fostering innovation that transcends traditional silos through collaborative engineering excellence.

Hackathon Specs

Problem Statements

Smart Infrastructure

Develop automated structural health monitoring systems using distributed sensor networks for aging urban architecture.

Energy Efficiency

Design intelligent HVAC control algorithms that integrate real-time occupancy data with renewable energy fluctuations.

Autonomous Logistics

Optimize last-mile delivery routes using interdisciplinary hardware solutions in dense civil environments.

Frequently Queried

help Who can participate?
expand_more

Undergraduate students from any engineering background with a passion for interdisciplinary innovation. Cross-branch teams are highly encouraged.

help Is there a registration fee?
expand_more

Registration for Civithon 2026 is free for all qualified student teams. However, selection is based on the initial conceptual abstract submission.

help Team size requirements?
expand_more

Teams must consist of 2 to 4 members. Interdepartmental teams are compulsory - at least 1 student must be from a different department.
